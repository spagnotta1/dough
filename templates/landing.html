Everything in one place, finally

Five things that usually live in five different apps — and the one that reads all of them together.

Transaction management

Every charge sorted into a category automatically, with rules you can teach when it guesses wrong. Search, filter, bulk-edit, and undo a whole import in one click.

Account aggregation

Link banks, brokerages and crypto exchanges once. Balances, holdings and transactions refresh on their own — or on demand, when you want to see something now.

Investment tracking

Shares, average cost, live prices and gain/loss per position — plus allocation and net-worth charts that redraw themselves after every sync.

AI financial assistant

Ask Dough anything about your own money — "why was last month expensive?", "can I afford this?" — and get an answer grounded in your actual figures rather than general advice.

Household collaboration

Invite the people you actually share money with. Everyone in a household sees the same accounts — and no household can see another's, ever.

Budget intelligence

Set a limit and Dough watches it for you — flagging the categories drifting over before the month ends, not after.

It tells you what changed, not just what happened

A list of transactions is a record. Dough reads the record and reports the story: the subscription that quietly went up, the category running 40% hot, the bill landing on Thursday that your balance has not accounted for.

  • Cash-flow forecast with a runway in months
  • Recurring bills detected, not typed in
  • Unusual charges surfaced for review
  • A net-worth line that goes back as far as your data does

Encouraging, never preachy

Going over budget is a fact to work with, not a failing. Dough is warm without being cute, direct without being alarming, and he will never use fear to get your attention — which, in a product that knows what you earn and what you owe, is the whole difference.

  • One voice across chat, dashboard and portfolio review
  • Sixteen themes, and it remembers which one is yours
  • Works on the phone as well as it does on the desk
